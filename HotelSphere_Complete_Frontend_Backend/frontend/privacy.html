HotelSphere respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes the types of information that may be collected when you use our website, create an account or make a hotel reservation. It also explains how that information may be used and protected.

1. Introduction

This policy applies to customers who access HotelSphere through its website and related booking services.

By using the platform, you acknowledge that information may be processed as described in this policy and under applicable law.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, the platform may collect the following categories of information.

Personal Information

Full name, email address and mobile number provided during registration.

Booking Information

Reservation details, room selection, check-in dates, check-out dates and number of guests.

Payment Information

Payment status, transaction references and relevant payment records.

Technical Information

Browser type, device information, access logs and basic usage information where collected.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information may be used for legitimate platform operations, including:

  • Creating and maintaining customer accounts.
  • Processing hotel reservations.
  • Managing cancellations and booking updates.
  • Maintaining customer and payment records.
  • Providing customer support.
  • Improving website functionality and security.
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations.

4. Booking Information

When a customer makes a reservation, the platform may process information such as:

  • Customer identification details.
  • Selected hotel and room information.
  • Reservation and booking reference.
  • Check-in and check-out dates.
  • Guest count and booking status.

This information helps manage reservations and provide booking-related services.

5. Payment Information

HotelSphere may maintain payment-related records such as transaction references, amounts, payment methods and payment status.

Actual payment card details should be processed through an appropriately secured payment provider. The platform should not store sensitive card verification data such as CVV.

Never share your card PIN, OTP, password or banking credentials with anyone claiming to represent HotelSphere.

6. Cookies and Technology

The website may use browser storage or cookies to support functionality and improve the customer experience.

Examples include:

  • Remembering a customer's selected light or dark theme.
  • Maintaining necessary session information where implemented.
  • Supporting website performance and user preferences.

Browser settings may allow you to control certain cookies and storage features.

7. Information Sharing

Personal information should only be shared where necessary for legitimate operations or where legally required.

Possible recipients may include:

  • Authorized hotel personnel responsible for managing reservations.
  • Payment service providers involved in processing transactions.
  • Technical service providers supporting platform operations.
  • Government or regulatory authorities where required by law.

Information should not be sold to third parties as a business practice.

8. Data Security

Appropriate technical and organizational safeguards should be used to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or loss.

  • Secure server-side authentication.
  • Proper password hashing.
  • Restricted database access.
  • Secure communication using HTTPS in production.
  • Monitoring and appropriate security logging.

No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security. Customers should also protect their login credentials.

9. Data Retention

Personal information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide services, maintain transaction records, resolve disputes and comply with applicable legal requirements.

When information is no longer required, it should be securely deleted or anonymized where appropriate and legally permitted.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, customers may have rights relating to their personal information.

  • Request access to personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion where legally applicable.
  • Raise concerns about how information is used.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Requests may be subject to identity verification and applicable legal requirements.

11. Children's Privacy

HotelSphere is not intended to independently collect personal information from children without appropriate legal authorization. Where required, a parent or lawful guardian should be involved.

12. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the platform develops or legal requirements change. The latest version and revision date will be published on this page.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions or privacy-related requests, you may contact the HotelSphere support team.
