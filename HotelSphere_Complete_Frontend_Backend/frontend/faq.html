Reservations & Booking

Visit the Explore Hotels page, select your preferred room, choose check-in and check-out dates, enter guest information and follow the checkout process.

Check your My Bookings section for the latest reservation status. A booking is confirmed only after successful processing by the booking system.

Modification availability depends on the booking status and hotel policy. Contact support with your booking reference for assistance.

Your booking reference should be available on the confirmation page and inside My Bookings after the reservation is successfully created.

Payments & Billing

HotelSphere is designed to support online payment, UPI and pay-at-hotel options where enabled. Actual availability depends on the configured payment system.

Once invoice generation is enabled, you can access the invoice from the booking confirmation page or My Bookings section.

Check your payment provider's transaction status before retrying. If the amount was deducted but the booking is not confirmed, contact support with your transaction reference.

The checkout summary should show applicable taxes and charges separately before confirmation. Always review the final amount before completing payment.

Cancellations & Refunds

Open My Bookings, select the relevant reservation and use the cancellation option if available. Cancellation must follow the applicable hotel policy.

Refund eligibility depends on the selected cancellation policy, payment status and applicable charges. Contact support for your specific reservation.

Refund processing time depends on the payment provider and banking channel. The actual timeline should be communicated after refund approval.

Same-day cancellation depends on the room's policy. Some reservations may have restrictions or cancellation charges. Check your booking conditions first.

Account & Security
